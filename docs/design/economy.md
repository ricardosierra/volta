# Economy Design & Simulation

## Currencies
- **Sparks**: Soft currency. Earned by playing and completing challenges.
- **Prisms**: Hard currency. Earned by achievements or IAP.

## Balance Targets
- Casual player (5 matches/day): ~260 Sparks/day (approx 7,800/month).
- Hardcore player (20 matches/day): ~750 Sparks/day (approx 22,500/month).

## Prices (Proposed)
- Common Avatar: 1,500 Sparks
- Rare Frame: 5,000 Sparks
- Epic Title: 15,000 Sparks
