## Short Description
Fast-paced multiplayer territory capture. Survive the neon grid!

## Long Description
Volta is a lightning-fast multiplayer game where you enclose territory on a neon grid to defeat opponents. Draw your arc, close your seal, and trap the competition before they trap you. 

Features:
- Deterministic multiplayer for low-latency competition
- Minimalist neon aesthetic
- Multiple arenas and game modes
