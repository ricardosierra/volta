# Volta Privacy Policy

## Data Collection
Volta collects telemetry data (device model, OS version, performance metrics, and in-game events) solely for game balancing and crash reporting. 

## Opt-out
You may opt-out of all telemetry collection in `Settings > Privacy`. Opting out will delete your device identifier from our servers.

## Account Data
If you link an external account (Google Play Games, Apple Game Center), we store your Player ID and Nickname to provide Cloud Save functionality. We do not sell this data.
